Respect and ranks

How do I earn Respect?

Log real things you did — a workout, a shift, a home-cooked meal, a sunset. Each log earns Respect, daily missions add a bonus, and streaks multiply it. Respect climbs you through seven tiers, from Nobody to Icon.

What does “verified” mean?

A log is verified when there’s proof: an Apple Health workout or steps, a fresh photo, or a check-in at a gym or job site you saved. Verified logs earn more, and boards rank verified Respect only so they stay fair.

Why did a log earn less than I expected?

To keep it about real life, repeats in one category earn less each time, each category has a daily cap, and there’s a daily ceiling. The log screen shows the exact maths before you save.

Can I buy Respect?

No. Respect is never for sale. Respect Cash only buys looks.

Apple Health and Apple Watch

My steps or workouts aren’t showing up

Open the app — it syncs every time it comes to the front. Check Settings › Privacy & Security › Health › Everyday Respect and make sure Steps and Workouts are on. Workouts from before you installed the app don’t count, and walks are covered by steps.

How does the Apple Watch app work?

It shows your rank, streak and missions, and lets you log with two taps. Logs are sent to your iPhone, which does the maths. If your iPhone is out of range the log is saved and counts on the day you tapped it once they reconnect (within two days).

Purchases

Restore Pro on a new device

Open the Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Cancel or change a subscription

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions › Everyday Respect. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. If you’re on the free trial, the app reminds you the day before it ends.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Crews, 1v1s and partner jobs

Why do I need Game Center and iCloud?

Crews and 1v1s use your Game Center nickname so you don’t need another account, and run on Apple’s iCloud. Sign in under Settings › Game Center and Settings › [your name] › iCloud.

My partner can’t see the jobs I asked for

They need to accept the invite link you sent and be signed in to iCloud. Results refresh when the app is opened.

Your data

How do I delete everything?

Settings › Delete all my data erases the app’s data on your iPhone and the records you created in iCloud. See the privacy policy for details.
