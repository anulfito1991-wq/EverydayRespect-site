Privacy policy

Effective November 24, 2026 · Last updated November 24, 2026

The short version

  • No account, no sign-up, no ads, no third-party analytics or tracking.
  • Your logs, journal, moods, places and photo checks stay on your iPhone (and your own Apple Watch).
  • Apple Health data stays on your iPhone. The one exception is opt-in: if you join a steps or workouts 1v1, that race's total is shared with your rival.
  • Crews, crew battles and 1v1 challenges are opt-in and show your Game Center nickname and scores to other players.
  • You can delete everything from the app: Settings › Delete all my data.

Who we are

Everyday Respect is made by Anulfo Acosta, an independent developer (“we”). Contact details are at the bottom of this page.

What stays on your device

Everything you log — activities, quantities, moods, journal lines (“Best part of today”), missions, streaks, rank, Respect Cash, looks, saved places and settings — is stored in a database inside the app on your iPhone. iOS encrypts it at rest, and it is included in your device backup if you have backups turned on. We cannot see it.

The app keeps a few on-device usage counters (for example, how many times a log was made) so future versions can understand retention. They never leave your iPhone and never contain what you wrote.

Apple Health

If you connect Apple Health, Everyday Respect reads your step count and workouts so they log themselves as verified Respect. It does not write to Apple Health. Health data is processed and stored on your iPhone and is not sent to us or used for advertising.

The 1v1 exception: if you choose to start or accept a 1v1 challenge measured in steps or workouts, the total for that race (for example, “41,200 steps” or “3 workouts”) is shared with your rival through Apple’s iCloud (see “Crews and challenges”). The app tells you this before you start or accept. Nothing else from Health is shared. You can revoke access any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Health › Everyday Respect.

Camera and photos (proof)

If you prove a log with a photo, the app computes a small fingerprint of the image on your iPhone to stop the same photo being reused. Only the fingerprint is kept; the photo itself is never stored or uploaded. Photos picked from your library are read once, without access to the rest of your library.

Location (check-ins)

If you save a gym or job site, the app checks your location once, only when you tap to check in, to confirm you are within about 150 m. Your location and saved places stay on your iPhone. There is no background location tracking.

Game Center

Leaderboards, friends and achievements use Apple’s Game Center. Boards show your Game Center nickname and avatar, and the scores you earn (only verified Respect, unless you turn on the total board). Game Center is run by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Crews and challenges (optional)

If you join or found a crew, start a crew battle or play a 1v1 challenge, the app stores these records in the app’s public iCloud database (Apple CloudKit), where other players can read them:

WhatShared
Your player recordGame Center nickname, an anonymous Game Center player ID, your tier, streak, chosen title, and this week’s and last week’s verified Respect
CrewsCrew name and emblem (picked from lists — never typed), invite code, founder’s player ID
Crew battlesWhich two crews race in which week
1v1 challengesBoth nicknames and player IDs, the metric (steps, workouts or verified Respect), dates, and each player’s score for that race
Hall of FameIf you reach the top tier: your nickname, title and the date

Nothing from your journal, moods, places, photos or individual logs is ever shared. There is no chat and no free text.

Partner jobs (optional)

If you invite a partner, the two of you share a private iCloud space that only you two can see. It holds your display names and the jobs you ask each other for (title, optional note, size and status). It lives in the inviter’s own iCloud account, not on any server of ours. Either of you can leave at any time; the inviter can close the household, which deletes it for both.

Apple Watch

The Apple Watch app shows your rank, streak and missions and lets you log from your wrist. It talks directly to your own iPhone; nothing passes through a server.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone (a daily nudge and a heads-up if your streak is at risk). There is no push server.

Purchases

Everyday Respect Pro subscriptions and Respect Cash packs are sold by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles payment and billing under its own privacy policy. We never receive your name, email or payment details — only the confirmation StoreKit gives the app on your device.

Feedback email

If you tap “Send Feedback”, your Mail app opens a draft to us containing only the app version, build, iOS version, device model and language. You see it before you send it. Nothing you logged is included.

Deleting your data

Settings › Delete all my data erases everything on your iPhone and deletes the records you created in iCloud (your player record, Hall of Fame entry, challenge entries, challenges you sent and crews you founded) and leaves any partner household. If you are offline, the iCloud part is retried the next time you open the app. Deleting the app removes the on-device data. Game Center scores and achievements are managed by Apple in your Game Center settings.

You can also export your logs as a file from Settings (photos and locations are never included).

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR or US state privacy laws) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to processing of your personal data. Most of it is already under your control in the app. For anything else, email us and we will respond within 30 days. Data shared through crews and challenges is processed to provide the features you turned on (performance of a contract). We do not sell or share personal data for advertising, and we do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Children

Everyday Respect is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Not medical advice

Everyday Respect is a motivation and habit app. It does not provide medical advice. Check with a professional before starting a new exercise routine.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top and note it in the app’s release notes. Any new kind of data sharing will be opt-in.
